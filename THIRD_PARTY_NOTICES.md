## CodexBar

Source: https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar

Reference commit: `5de8b9ccfdcf3ed13d7c67e3639a2dd18d11230f`

QuotaMenu adapts the Cursor local-auth cookie construction and usage-summary mapping, Copilot quota mapping and request headers, and Antigravity quota-summary/CLI report shapes and local endpoint protocol. These projections were rewritten to fit QuotaMenu's injected data sources and repositories; CodexBar's application, branding, browser-cookie scanning and account management are not included.
